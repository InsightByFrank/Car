## 📖 Description  
This Power BI dashboard presents an interactive and analytical overview of car rental performance, helping rental agencies and operations managers monitor rentals, revenue, utilization, customer behavior, and profit. It tracks overdue and damaged returns, maps fleet performance across branches, and highlights cost vs return insights across vehicle models.

Built with advanced DAX calculations, drillthroughs, and slicers, it transforms routine rental data into strategic intelligence for better decision-making and customer experience optimization.

## ⚙️ Power BI Tools & Features  
- **DAX Measures** – Profit margin, overdue rate, ADR, utilization %  
- **Drillthrough Pages** – Vehicle- and customer-level breakdowns  
- **Custom Tooltips** – Extra metrics without cluttering visuals  
- **Slicers** – Filter by car type, branch, return status, or customer segment  
- **KPI Cards & Alerts** – Auto-flagging overdue/damaged cars  
- **Clustered Charts & Line Visuals** – Rental trends and cost comparison  

---

## ❓ Business Questions Answered  
- Which car models bring the highest returns?  
- What are the rental and return trends over time?  
- How frequently are cars returned late or damaged?  
- Which branches underperform, and why?  
- How can utilization be improved to reduce idle time?  
- What’s the overall profitability by car type or category?

---

## 📌 Insights & Observations  
- 🚗 Sedans and SUVs dominate both revenue and rental frequency  
- 🔧 SUVs yield higher income but incur more maintenance costs  
- 📍 Urban branches (Lagos, Abuja) outperform rural ones by 35%  
- 🔁 Cars idle more than 5 days mostly in suburban areas  
- 🛠️ Hyundai & Toyota models perform well across revenue and durability metrics  

---

## 📉 Visual Analytics & Layout  
- 🗺️ **Fleet Map** – Revenue and utilization by branch  
- 📅 **Calendar Timeline** – Pickup vs return flow  
- 📊 **Bar Charts** – Cost vs Revenue by car model  
- 🍩 **Donut Chart** – Overdue Return Rate + Damage Heat  
- 🧾 **Customer Table** – Loyalty tier, history, and contact group  
- 📈 **Utilization Line** – Trend by month and car type

## 💡 Actionable Insights  
1. Monitor overdue returns to reduce idle time between bookings  
2. Incentivize loyal, no-damage customers with reward tiers  
3. Launch predictive maintenance alerts based on return trends  
4. Report idle vehicles >5 days and realign availability per region  

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations  
- Use tiered pricing linked to utilization trends  
- Introduce real-time alerts for overdue returns  
- Expand branches where utilization exceeds 90%  
- Segment customers for personalized rental upsells

- 🔄 Higher rental volume through improved utilization
